From curiosity to evidence

A clearer way into research.

Research starts with a focused question and a careful look at what is already known.

01

Frame a question

Define the problem, scope, constraints and what a useful answer would establish.

02

Review the literature

Identify relevant papers, compare methods and distinguish findings from assumptions.

03

Plan an investigation

Choose measures, consider risks and limitations, and map the next experiment or analysis.

Build with intent

Turn ideas into engineering work.

Strong projects make the problem, design decisions and tests visible from the start.

⌑

Design brief

Describe the need, users, requirements, constraints and measures of success.

⌁

Model & simulate

Use sketches, calculations and simulations to test assumptions before building.

◈

Prototype & evaluate

Build in stages, record results and improve the design based on evidence.

Build your foundation

Learn in a useful sequence.

Use this general path as a starting point, then adapt it to the field and project you care about.

STAGE 01

Core concepts

Strengthen mathematics, scientific reasoning and the fundamental principles of your chosen field.

STAGE 02

Tools & methods

Practice with relevant software, measurement methods, modeling and technical communication.

STAGE 03

Applied project

Combine concepts and tools in a scoped project, document your decisions and evaluate results.
